Interpretation: On the held-out May–August 2018 test period, the Logistic Regression model achieved 76.41% recall — meaning it identified approximately 76% of orders that were actually late, using only information available at order approval. Precision is low (6.08%) due to class imbalance (~93% of orders are on-time). The Random Forest achieved near-random AUC (0.52) on this test window due to a temporal distribution shift (test period had lower late rates than training). These results apply to the specific test period and should not be interpreted as guaranteed future performance. The model is intended as an early-warning mechanism, not a delivery guarantee.
